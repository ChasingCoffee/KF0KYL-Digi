### What's new in 1.2.3

**Click, then Enter**
- **Single-click a line** in Band Activity or Rx Frequency to select it. It gets a green border, and only one line is selected at a time across both windows.
- **Enter** calls the selected station, the same as a double-click. **↑ ↓**, **Page Up** and **Page Down** move the selection. Double-click still works, and **Esc** still halts Tx.
- The selection stays on its line as new decodes come in. A small key hint shows under the window; click its **×** to hide it for good.

**Max transmit power**
- **Tools → Max transmit power...** sets the most watts you want to send. Before each transmission KFØKYL Digi reads the radio's power setting over CAT. If it's above your maximum, nothing is transmitted, Tx is turned off and you're told to turn the power down. Handy after a session of 100 W SSB.
- **0** means no limit (the default). Radios that can't report their power setting are never blocked.
