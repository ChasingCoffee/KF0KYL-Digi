### What's new in 1.2.1

- **Worked stations on the map:** stations already in your log on the band you're on show in their own colour. Click the Worked chip in the legend to switch between *Worked before*, *Worked today* and hidden.
- **Worked grids:** a new map layer shades the grid squares you've logged on the band (or just today's).
- **Heard me:** lines to every station that reported hearing you in the last 30 minutes (PSK Reporter), coloured strong, fair or weak, with a key on the map.
- **New Band Activity filters:** *Show only new DXCC and grids on this band* and *Hide signals weaker than…*, at the top of the Filters menu.
- **Sound when a QSO is logged:** Tools > Sound when a QSO is logged… plays a .wav of your choice.
- **Update notices:** KF0KYL Digi now tells you when a new version is out and downloads it for you (Help > Check for updates). On Windows it closes and starts the installer.
- New installs start in the Big Band Activity layout.
