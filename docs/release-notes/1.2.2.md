### What's new in 1.2.2

**Conditions window**
- **New top bar:** Station, NCDXF beacons, X-ray, a compact **Space weather** box (SFI, SSN, K, A, X-ray, wind and Bz, with units and a word on what each reading means) and **the Sun**. The map is taller, and the DX box has room for a big QRZ photo.
- **The Sun:** sharper 1024-pixel NASA SDO pictures, the whole disk with flares and prominences round the edge, Az/El and next rise or set in the corners. **Double-click** it to pick a picture, play the last two days as a movie, or open the **gray line tool**.
- **QRZ in the DX box:** the station's name, place and photo are now in the Conditions window. Photos are never trimmed, and the box keeps one size so VOACAP doesn't jump around.
- **Map:** Flat, Azimuthal and Robinson projections; CQ zone, ITU zone, tropics and beam-heading grids; colour the map by **MUF** (live from prop.kc2g.com, with VOACAP's prediction standing in if it can't be reached) or **D-region absorption** (NOAA). **Fit** now shows pole to pole.
- **Heard me live:** PSK Reporter reports arrive as they happen, for this band or all bands.
- **Map colours:** double-click any colour in the map's key to change it.
- **Stations keep their real grid:** grids from any message, the DX Grid box or QRZ are remembered, so stations aren't dropped in the middle of their country. Country-only guesses show as a faint ring.
- **News ticker:** ARRL, AMSAT and DX-World headlines, plus news about KFØKYL Digi itself.

**Main window**
- **New waterfall views:** Classic, Waterfall, 3D and Both, new Digi and Nebula palettes, and **Calls on signals** to show each decoded call on its signal. Double-click a call to answer it. All the controls sit in one toolbar that hides with one button.
- **Colour key** beside Band Activity: double-click a colour to change its highlight and text colour.
- **Filters** now hide stations in Rx Frequency, on the map and on the waterfall too (never your own QSO).
- **Max tries are hard stops:** the count no longer resets when the program re-enables Tx in the middle of a run.
- **Logs that count as worked:** add other ADIF logs so their QSOs count as worked.

**Help menu**
- The new **KFØKYL Digi User Guide**, online and installed with the program. Updated keyboard and mouse help that scrolls instead of running off the screen. **Report a problem or request a feature**, and the WSJT-X mode guides in their own submenu.

**Fixes**
- Selected tabs and buttons no longer cut off their text.
- Each decode is filtered on its own (a filtered station could hide the next decodes in the same batch).
- Download samples and the menu-bar logo are gone.
